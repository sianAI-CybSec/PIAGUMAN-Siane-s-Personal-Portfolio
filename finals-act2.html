Integrals Activity 2

Score: 14/14

Description

Author: Crisiane Josef A. Caranto  |  Date: September 21, 2026

An activity to apply Integral Calculus with trigonometric functions

Objectives
  • Apply trigonometric derivatives to integrate trigonometric functions
  • Demostrate the concept of integrals in trigonometric functions
Reflection

With this activity, I learned the importance of learning the derivatives of trigonometric functions first before integration. Because with integrating trigonometric functions, it is basically reversing the differentiation process - which now I find fascinating on how I can use past lessons to help me solve integrals.
