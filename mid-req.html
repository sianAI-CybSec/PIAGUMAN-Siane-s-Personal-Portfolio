Midterms Req: Video Tutorial on Differential Calculus

Score: 47/55

Description

Author: Crisiane Josef A. Caranto  |  Date: August 27, 2026

A 5-member groupwork where we simulate teaching Differential Calculus and its fundamental rules to students using a Little Einsteins theme.

Objectives
  • Create a video tutorial that showcases the concept of Derivatives
  • Illustrate the different formulas used in finding derivatives of various functions
  • Demonstrate at least five (5) specific examples of varying difficulty (Easy, Average, Difficult) on the use of each formula
Reflection

In all honesty, I find our group's performance for this project lacking since we got a low score of 47 out of 50. For me, I know that I can finish the project on my own since I already did seminars and taught students about various topics in different schools. So I thought that this will be something I can finish on my own. However, I realized that it is best for the group to utilize their skills and do what they want. With this, we finished the work much faster and considerably okay - but I noticed that there were some errors along the way. I went with it since this is what my groupmates did. At the end of the day, what I learned most is that working with others is fun. And despite gaining a low score, teamwork is the best thing I learned during the project since we got to know a bit more of each other's skills (which I can use for my future profession). And, we also learned a lot from our mistakes.
