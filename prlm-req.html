Infographics on the Limits and Functions in Computing

Score: 50/50

Description

Author: Crisiane Josef A. Caranto  |  Date: July 13, 2026

This infographic entitled 'THE_FUNCTIONS_OF_COMPUTING.html' presents how functions and limits are applied to different disciplines in the field of computing. From designing computer systems and Artificial Intelligence, to Data Science and Analytics and Internet, Web, and Security, functions and limits are applied to innovate and further improve upon our current technologies not only to make them more efficient, but also better for user experience.

Objectives
  • Show how Calculus is used in real-life applications
  • Present the importance of limits to computing
  • Shoowcase the various fields of computing and their connection to Calculus
Reflection

Throughout the project, I first thought that Calculus is disconnected to my field, Cybersecurity, and to the rest of computing. But in making the infographic, I realized that mathematics, especially Calculus, really is the language of everything. Since I mostly work on programming and cybersecurity, calculus becomes a really helpful tool to me especially in the field of encryption where the time of cracking the lock can reach a limit - especially on public and private keys that relies on large prime numbers. With this, I really loved that I can use Calculus to help me in my future profession.
