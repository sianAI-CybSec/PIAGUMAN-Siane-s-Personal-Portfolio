Basic Differentiation Formulas Activity

Score: 20/20

Description

Author: Crisiane Josef A. Caranto  |  Date: July 30, 2026

A practice on applying the basic differentiation formulas, focusing on the Power Rule and Sum Rule.

Objectives
  • Apply basic differentiation to various functions
  • Demonstrate the Power Rule and Sum Rule
Reflection

The Basic Differentiation Formulas activity made me learn more about the application of the Power Rule and Sum Rule in not only basic functions, but also to functions with rational and negative exponents. Building upon the previous introduction to derivatives, I know that my ability to differentiate functions improved by being exposed to relatively more complicated functions.
