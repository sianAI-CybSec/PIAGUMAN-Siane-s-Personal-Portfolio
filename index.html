With the quiz, I have considered myself proficient in understanding the functions and limits in Calculus. Score: 50/50 July 13, 2026

Throughout the project, I first thought that Calculus is disconnected to my field, Cybersecurity, and to the rest of computing. This prelim exam showed me that practicing in Calculus resulted in better performance and better competence in applying functions and limits concepts to various problems.
